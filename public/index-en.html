Key Features of the QNB Token Watch for Protecting Your Bank Account

Highest levels of cybersecurity:
The watch generates dynamic and temporary security codes (OTP) that are used only once per transaction, making it impossible to hack or reuse.

Comprehensive protection against fraud:
It forms a strong line of defense that prevents malicious attempts such as social engineering and phishing, as completing any transaction requires direct approval through the watch.

Securing all transfers and financial transactions:
Whether you are making a domestic or international transfer, or adding a new beneficiary, the watch ensures full encryption of the process and verification of your identity.

Protecting your money around the clock (24/7):
It gives you complete peace of mind when managing your bank accounts online from anywhere and at any time without the fear of leaking your login data.

Ease and speed of use:
Quick and direct connection to your QNB National Bank account to secure your transaction with the press of a button through the watch without any complications.
